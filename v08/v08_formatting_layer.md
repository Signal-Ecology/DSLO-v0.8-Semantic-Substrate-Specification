## **3. DSLO‑Native Formatting Surfaces**
The Formatting Layer defines seven lawful DSLO‑native surface types:

### **`.geom` — Geometry Surfaces**  
Define geometric primitives, curvature envelopes, and manifold alignment rules.

### **`.domain` — Domain Surfaces**  
Define the 30‑domain, 5‑meta‑class DSLO classification system.

### **`.mapping` — Mapping Surfaces**  
Define lawful cross‑surface transitions, mapping invariants, and multi‑surface alignment.

### **`.invariant` — Invariant Surfaces**  
Define invariant families, continuity bands, and stability envelopes.

### **`.schema` — Schema Surfaces**  
Define DSLO‑native serialization rules and legality envelopes for machine ingestion.

### **`.registry` — Registry Surfaces**  
Define registry anchors, continuity rules, and lawful lineage declarations.

### **`.matrix` — Matrix Surfaces**  
Define multi‑surface mapping matrices, including **Matrix‑3** for v0.8.

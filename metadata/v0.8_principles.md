
# **DSLO v0.8 Principles**  
**September, 2026**  

**Discipline‑Level Meaning Systems (DSLO)**

---

*DSLO v0.8 establishes the lawful architecture for* **federated meaning systems**.

---

## **1. Substrate‑First Construction**
All DSLO geometry continues to originate in the substrate.  
Substrate surfaces must be **authored** or **substrate‑generated**, **never derived**.  
Federated geometry may only activate after substrate completion and lawful generator traversal.

---

## **2. Deterministic Inheritance**
Every v0.8 surface must inherit deterministically from v0.7 substrate roots.

Inheritance must preserve **invariants**, **legality**, **continuity**, and **federated stability**.

---

## **3. Unified Substrate Manifold**
All geometry remains within the Unified Substrate Manifold.

## **4. Lawful Federated Geometry Propagation**
**Meaning**, **curvature**, **drift**, **load**, **legality**, **identity**, and **domain‑class geometry** must propagate lawfully.

## **5. Invariant Stability**
Invariant families govern **substrate stability**, **manifold continuity**, **operator legality**, **domain‑class geometry**, **mapping geometry**, and **federated behavior**.

## **6. Domain‑Class Geometry**
